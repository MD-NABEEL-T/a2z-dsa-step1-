SELECTION SORT
    -FIND MIN AND PLACE IT TO LEFT 
    - finds the smallest,swap and move to the next index 
    eg: compare 23 with everyone and since 4 is smallest swap it at index 0 and then move to next element 

BUBBLE SORT
    -FIND MAX AND PUSH IT TO RIGHT
    -compare the element with adjacent element and then swap until the max reaches to the last
    eg: compare 23 with 6 then swap until the the 45 reaches to end then start again from first index but till index-1

INSERTION SORT
    -COMPARE NEXT ELEMENT WITH THE SORTED ELEMENTS
    -compare next index ,sort it and move to the next index and compare itwith the sorted values
