Privacy & how SAHAY AI works (simulation)

  • What is analysed: words you type/speak, pauses, and self-reported feelings — only to estimate stress/trauma priority.
  • Where: on-device first; only with your consent is a summary shared with the NHAA operator.
  • No judgement: the AI does not decide your case — it only suggests next steps for the human operator.
  • Your control: remain anonymous, skip questions, delete data, or ask for human-only support.
